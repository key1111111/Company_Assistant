## Annual Leave Entitlement
Employees are entitled to 25 working days of annual leave per year.

## Leave Approval Process
All leave requests must be approved by direct managers.

## Carry Over Policy
Unused leave may be carried over up to 5 days.

## Sick Leave Allowance
Employees receive up to 10 paid sick leave days annually.

## Maternity Leave
Maternity leave is granted according to legal requirements.

## Paternity Leave
Paternity leave is available for eligible employees.

## Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave may be granted under special circumstances.

## Public Holidays
Official public holidays are observed according to country regulations.

## Leave Request Notice
Leave requests should be submitted at least 7 days in advance.

## Emergency Leave
Emergency leave is available for unforeseen situations.

## Leave Balance Tracking
Leave balances are tracked in the HR system.

## Probation Period Leave
Limited leave applies during probation period.

## Remote Work During Leave
Working during approved leave is not permitted.

## Leave Encashment
Unused leave encashment depends on employment contract.

## Medical Leave Certification
Medical certificates may be required for extended sick leave.

## Sabbatical Policy
Long-term employees may apply for sabbatical leave.

## Holiday Blackout Periods
Certain business periods restrict leave approvals.

## Leave Overlap Policy
Team scheduling prevents excessive overlapping leave.

## Partial Day Leave
Half-day leave requests are supported.

## Exit Leave Settlement
Remaining leave is settled upon employment termination.
