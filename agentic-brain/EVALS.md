# questions and expected answers

- Q:Are training materials available for new users?
- A:## Training Materials
    Video and document training materials are available.
    Source: support.md

- Q:Can unused leave days be carried over to the next year?
- A:## Carry Over Policy
    Unused leave may be carried over up to 5 days.
    Source: leave-rules.md

- Q:How many annual leave days do employees receive each year?
- A:## Annual Leave Entitlement
    Employees are entitled to 25 working days of annual leave per year.
    Source: leave-rules.md

- Q:Does the system include bank reconciliation features?
- A:## Bank Reconciliation Tool
    Matches internal records with bank statements.
    Source: products.md

- Q:Can I export financial reports to Excel or PDF?
- A:## Data Export Options
    Reports can be exported in PDF, Excel, and CSV formats.
    Source: faq.md

- Q:What is the expected response time for a support ticket?
- A:## Response Time
    Standard response time is within 24 business hours.
    Source: support.md

- Q:Does the accounting software support multiple currencies?
- A:## Multi-Currency Support
    Supports transactions in multiple currencies with conversion.
    Source: products.md
